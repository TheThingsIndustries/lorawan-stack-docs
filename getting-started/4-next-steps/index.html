Step 4: Next steps

Now that you are familiar with the basics of LoRaWAN and you’ve setup your first network and experimented with an end device and a gateway, this guide gives you some options on what to do next.

  1. Add other LoRaWAN end devices to send LoRaWAN traffic to your network.
  2. Learn how to get the most out of The Things Stack.
  3. Check out the Glossary for a disambiguation of some of these terms and FAQ.
